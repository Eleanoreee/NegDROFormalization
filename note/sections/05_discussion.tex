\section{Discussion and Scope}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Relation to the official NegDRO algorithms}

The verified theorem and the official algorithms answer related but distinct
questions.  The official algorithms form empirical environmental risks from a
fixed data set, solve an inner maximization, and select an iterate using a
stationarity criterion.  The extension in this note targets population risks
through a fresh conditional law and updates both variables online.

This comparison does not imply a uniform sample- or computation-complexity
ordering.  In the official method, an iteration may revisit a fixed data set.
Here $T$ counts both online updates and fresh-law rounds.  The present Lean
development is therefore not a literal formalization of official
arXiv:2412.11850v3 Algorithm~1.

\subsection{What the fixed witness accomplishes}

The analytical weight $w^0$ separates identification from the algorithm's
dual optimization.  Spectral curvature at $w^0$ gives the primal direction
bound, while the comparator identity transfers the difference between $w^0$
and $w_t$ into a dual objective regret.  Exponentiated-gradient ascent controls
the cumulative comparator term even though an individual regret can be
negative.  The projected primal recursion then converts this cumulative
control into an expected squared-error bound.

The penalized and unpenalized calculations differ in two explicit places.
The dual oracle and its moment constant change from $G_{w,0}^2$ to
$G_{w,\mu}^2$, and the simplex norm comparison contributes the structural
bias $2\mu/\lambda$.  There is no accumulation into $2\mu T$ after averaging.

\subsection{Scope boundaries}

Target~A of the formalization is complete: the conditional theorem for the
stochastic extension is kernel checked relative to its stated hypotheses.
The following are genuine scope boundaries, not missing steps in that proof.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \emph{Sampling realization.}  The universal fresh conditional law
        \eqref{eq:fresh-conditional-law} is assumed.  The project does not
        construct a product probability space, a natural filtration, or an
        infinite independent stream.
  \item \emph{Reduced-form entry point.}  The final theorem begins with the
        corrected realized maps
        \eqref{eq:corrected-realized-X}--\eqref{eq:corrected-realized-Y}.
        Separate Lean lemmas justify structural elimination from
        \eqref{eq:sem} when the required inverse identity is supplied, but the
        final wrapper does not derive $G^\top$ from $B_{XX}$ and acyclicity.
  \item \emph{Expectation.}  The results are in expectation.  No
        high-probability theorem or adaptive concentration result is claimed.
  \item \emph{Output.}  The analysis controls the time average $D_T$ and the
        expected squared error of $\bar b_T$.  It does not establish a
        last-iterate guarantee.
  \item \emph{Identification bias.}  The term $B_{\rm id}$ remains unless
        $v_{\rm SEM}$ vanishes or is controlled through other structural
        information.  The theorem does not silently identify it with an
        optimization error.
  \item \emph{Algorithmic scope.}  This fresh-sample simultaneous projected
        SGD/EG method is not official v3 Algorithm~1 and does not verify exact
        empirical-risk maximization.
  \item \emph{Rate interface.}  The project proves explicit inequalities.  It
        does not add a formal \texttt{IsBigO} wrapper, and the RMS
        square-root consequence is not an expected-norm theorem.
\end{enumerate}

The fourth-moment, bounded-domain, spectral heterogeneity, sign, and
fresh-law conditions remain substantive theorem hypotheses.  Calling the
conditional proof complete does not mean that the theorem is assumption-free.

\subsection{Conclusion}

The corrected additive model, fixed spectral witness, conditional fresh-law
oracle, entropy geometry, and Euclidean projection fit into one closed proof
chain.  The resulting finite-time inequalities separate three effects:
initialization and online optimization, the structural identification term
$B_{\rm id}$, and---only in the penalized case---$2\mu/\lambda$.  This yields a
self-contained mathematical account of the finite-dimensional stochastic
extension and a precise boundary between that extension and the original
empirical NegDRO algorithms.
