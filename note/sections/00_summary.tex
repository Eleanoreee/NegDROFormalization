\begin{abstract}
This note develops a finite-dimensional online stochastic extension of
Negative-Weighted Distributionally Robust Optimization (NegDRO).  The method
combines nearest-point-projected stochastic descent in the prediction
parameter with exponentiated-gradient ascent in the environmental weights;
both updates may use the same fresh observation.  Under a corrected realized
additive-intervention reduced-form model, a universal fresh conditional law,
fourth-moment bounds, a bounded closed convex feasible set, and a fixed
spectral heterogeneity witness, we prove explicit finite-time and
averaged-iterate expected squared-error bounds.  The complete conditional
proof chain is verified in Lean~4 and Mathlib, including the realized model
bridge, oracle unbiasedness and integrability, Euclidean projection, entropy
regret, spectral curvature, and telescoping recursion.  With
$\eta_b=\eta_w=T^{-1/2}$, the optimization contribution is of order
$T^{-1/2}$ in expected squared error; the penalized result has the additional
structural bias $2\mu/\lambda$.  The analysis is based on a fixed comparator
and does not require independence between the two stochastic gradients.  It
is an extension developed in this note, not a literal formalization of the
empirical-risk exact-maximization Algorithm~1 in
arXiv:2412.11850v3~\cite{wang2026negdro}.
\end{abstract}
